Windows setup:
  The unmodified official OpenVPN Community 2.7.8-I001 x64 MSI is included.
  Its license notices are retained inside the MSI and installed OpenVPN folder.
  DropoVPN installs the OpenVPN engine and signed TAP-Windows6 network driver.
  OpenVPN is shared system software and is not removed with DropoVPN.
