A note about this preview

These installers are unsigned; macOS and Windows may display security warnings. On a Mac, choose Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security the first time; on Windows, choose More info → Run anyway. Live VPN routing and reconnection still need testing with your server. Use a VPN profile from a provider or IT team you trust. The first time the device mesh starts, your system may ask to allow incoming connections.

DropoVPN is a client, not a VPN service. OpenVPN 2.7.8 is included; you bring a working .ovpn profile from your provider or IT team. Starting a tunnel still requires administrator permission.

FOUR SMALL STEPS

Bring your own
connection.

No new account to create. Use the network and credentials you already have.

  1. 1

    Install once.

    Download DropoVPN for your computer. The Mac app contains its engine; Windows setup installs the engine and network driver for you.

  2. 2

    Import your .ovpn profile.

    Choose “Import profile” and select the file from your VPN provider or IT team. Keep any separate certificates alongside the file.

  3. 3

    Connect. Remember. Settle in.

    Enter your VPN credentials and choose “Remember me on this device.” Approve the system administrator prompt to start your tunnel.

  4. 4

    Bring your devices along.

    Open Device mesh, create a mesh, and join from your other devices with the invite. Share an address like localhost:3000 and open it on all of them.

GOOD TO KNOW

A little clarity.

What happens when my connection drops?

With automatic reconnection enabled, DropoVPN retries using the same session credentials. Retry delays increase up to 30 seconds. Rejected passwords pause the connection so you can correct them. If the engine exits and must restart, your computer may ask for administrator permission again. Disconnecting manually stops retries.

Where are my saved passwords stored?

On your computer, encrypted with Electron safeStorage using macOS Keychain or Windows DPAPI. DropoVPN supplies credentials to the local OpenVPN engine when needed. You can forget a saved password from the profile menu. Windows DPAPI does not protect against every program running as your Windows user.

Does it work with my existing VPN?

DropoVPN supports imported OpenVPN TUN profiles with username/password or certificate authentication. One-time codes, browser sign-in, proxy authentication, hardware tokens, TAP profiles, and connection blocks are not supported in this preview. Some provider-specific configurations may need a compatible export.

What happens when I uninstall DropoVPN?

On macOS, the engine is part of the app and is removed with it. On Windows, OpenVPN and its driver are shared system components and stay installed. You can remove OpenVPN separately in Windows Installed Apps if no other VPN application needs it.

Does DropoVPN include a kill switch?

No. Your profile controls routing, and traffic is not automatically blocked while reconnecting. A split-tunnel profile only routes the destinations it specifies through the VPN.

What can the DropoVPN relay see?

The relay connects your mesh devices when they can’t reach each other directly. Traffic between your devices is end-to-end encrypted with a key only they hold, so the relay passes it along without being able to read it. It does see which devices are connected, when, and how much data they exchange. You can run your own relay or turn relaying off in the app. The relay at dropovpn.samuelnimoh.com is a background service the app connects to, not a website.

Do I need the VPN to use the device mesh?

No. The device mesh and public URLs work on their own, with or without a VPN connection, and need no administrator permission. If your devices connect to the same OpenVPN server and it allows traffic between clients, they can use that network to connect directly.

Do public URLs need an account?

Cloudflare quick tunnels need no account: install cloudflared and DropoVPN gives you a random trycloudflare.com address. ngrok uses the account you’ve signed in to with its agent. Your own DropoVPN relay can serve addresses on your domain. Anyone with a public link can reach that address until you stop it.

Is it free and open source?

Yes. DropoVPN’s application source is available under the MIT license. The included OpenVPN components retain their own licenses. Download the runtime sources and build instructions. Read the source or report an issue on GitHub.
